FAQ

Questions brands ask

What kind of content do creators post?

Mostly faceless slideshows and short clips made from your brief. We prepare the content, captions and hashtags, so every post stays on-message.

How do you make sure views are real?

Creators verify ownership of each TikTok account, every video is matched to its account, and views are read directly from TikTok. Every video is checked before it counts.

What does it cost?

Every campaign is different. Send us your product and goal, and we come back with a proposal, from a small test to a large launch. Everything is included.

Can I see the results myself?

Yes. You get your own login with live views and videos per campaign, and you can pause a campaign any time.

Which countries and languages?

Creators post in their own language, including English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Turkish and Arabic.
