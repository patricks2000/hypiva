How to order UGC videos for your brand, step by step

You want real videos of real people using your product, for your socials or your ads. But how do you actually get them made? Here is how ordering UGC works, from first idea to the video running in your ad.

What do you get when you order UGC?

UGC (user generated content) is short video that looks like a normal customer made it: someone showing your product, trying it and saying what they think. You use these videos on your own channels, on your website or as ads on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

The difference with a commercial: UGC feels like a tip from a friend. That is why people stop scrolling for it more often and believe it faster.

How it works, in 5 steps

  1. Pick your goal and channel. Ads, your own TikTok, or a stronger product page? That decides length, format and tone.
  2. Brief. Say what your product does, who it is for and what people should remember. A good brief fits on one page.
  3. Choose a creator. Someone who fits your audience: age, style, language. Follower count matters little for UGC, the video is what counts.
  4. Ship the product and film. The creator really uses your product and usually films within a week.
  5. Feedback and delivery. You review the videos, ask for small changes and receive the files to use.

What do you need?

  • Your product (or access to your app or service).
  • One clear message, for example "vegan but still creamy" or "saves you 10 minutes a day".
  • Do's and don'ts: claims you don't want, brands that must not be on screen.
  • Where and how long you will use the video, so usage rights are clear.

How many videos do you need?

Start with three to five videos, with different hooks or different creators. With ads you never know upfront which one works best. Testing finds the winner, and then you make more versions of it.

What to look for in a UGC agency

  • Examples. Ask for real videos they made before.
  • Creators who fit your audience, including language.
  • Clear usage terms: organic, ads, for how long.
  • Revisions: how many rounds of feedback are included?
  • One point of contact, so you don't email ten creators at once.

Frequently asked questions

How fast will I get my UGC videos?

Usually within one to two weeks after the product arrives. The first videos are often ready within 7 days.

Can I use the videos in ads?

Yes, if you agree on it upfront. Write down which channels and for how long you may use them.

Does the creator need a lot of followers?

No. With UGC you pay for the video, not the reach. A creator with few followers can make a better video than someone with a big account.

How much does it cost to order UGC?

It depends on the number of videos, their length and how you use them. Read more in how much a UGC video costs.
