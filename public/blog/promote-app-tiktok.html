How to promote your app on TikTok with slideshows (2026)

You've seen them: TikToks with a few photos and some text, like "5 habits that changed my mornings" or "apps I use every day". These slideshows are one of the simplest ways to get an app noticed on TikTok. In this guide you'll learn why they work and how to use them for your app.

What is a TikTok slideshow?

A TikTok slideshow (also called a photo carousel) is a post made of several images that people swipe through, usually with short text on each slide. No filming, no editing: just a strong idea, a few visuals and clear text.

Why slideshows work so well for apps

  • They don't feel like ads. Your app shows up naturally in one of the slides, as a real tip, instead of as a commercial.
  • People read until the end. Viewers swipe through every slide, so your message really lands.
  • They're fast to make. No camera or studio needed, so you can test lots of ideas quickly.
  • One good idea can travel far. TikTok shows content to people who don't follow you yet, so even a small account can reach a big audience.

How to make a slideshow that promotes your app

  1. Start with a hook on slide 1. This decides whether people keep swiping. Think "I tried 5 budgeting apps, only one stuck" or "small habits that made me more productive".
  2. Give real value. Each slide should be a useful tip, insight or step, not a sales pitch.
  3. Place your app naturally. Put it in one of the middle or later slides, as part of the story: "this is the app I use to track it".
  4. Keep the text short. One idea per slide, readable in a second or two.
  5. Test many variations. Try different hooks and angles, then make more of what gets views.

Slideshow ideas for different types of apps

  • Habit and productivity apps: "Tiny habits that changed my year"
  • Fitness apps: "How I finally stuck to a workout routine"
  • Finance apps: "How I save €200 a month without noticing"
  • Language apps: "How I learned the basics of a new language in 3 months"
  • Sleep and wellness apps: "My night routine for falling asleep faster"

Post yourself or work with creators?

You can post slideshows on your own account, but new accounts often start slowly. Posting through several real creator accounts gives your app more chances to be seen, in more languages and by different audiences, and lets you learn faster which ideas work.

Frequently asked questions

How many slides should a TikTok slideshow have?

Usually somewhere between 4 and 8 slides works well: enough to tell a story, short enough that people swipe to the end.

Can I add music to a slideshow?

Yes. A fitting sound helps the slideshow feel like regular TikTok content.

How do I know if it works?

Look at views, how many people swipe to the end, and above all whether you see more downloads in the days after posting.
