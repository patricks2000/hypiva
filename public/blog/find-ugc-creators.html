How to find UGC creators for your brand

Good UGC starts with the right creator. But where do you find them, how do you know who is good, and what do you send them? This guide walks you through it step by step.

Where to find UGC creators

  • TikTok and Instagram. Search for hashtags like #ugccreator or #ugc together with your niche, such as "skincare" or "fitness".
  • Your own customers. People who already love your product often make the most honest videos. Check who tags you.
  • Creator platforms and marketplaces. There are platforms where creators offer UGC, but quality varies a lot.
  • An agency. An agency like Hypiva already has creators, ideas and a review process, so you skip the searching and checking.

What to look for in a creator

  1. The first seconds of their videos. Does the hook make you keep watching? That is the most important skill.
  2. Natural on camera. It should feel like a friend giving a tip, not like a commercial.
  3. Good light and clear sound. A phone is fine, but the image and audio need to be clean.
  4. Fit with your audience. Age, style and language should match the people you want to reach.
  5. Reliability. Do they reply quickly and deliver on time? Ask for examples and a short test first.

How to write a good brief

A brief tells the creator what you need, without taking away their own style. Keep it short and clear:

  • The product and the one thing that must come across, for example "100% plant-based" or "saves you 10 minutes a day".
  • 2 or 3 must-haves, such as the product clearly in view, a discount code or a key phrase.
  • An example hook and structure, as inspiration, not a script to read out.
  • What to avoid, such as medical claims or prices.
  • The format: vertical, length, and whether you also want a version without text and music.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Choosing on followers only. For UGC, the video matters more than the audience.
  • A brief that is too strict. If every word is fixed, the video stops feeling real.
  • No agreement on usage. Agree upfront where and how long you can use the video.
  • Only one video. Test several hooks and creators: the winner is often a surprise.

Frequently asked questions

How many UGC creators should I start with?

Two or three is a good start. That way you see quickly which style works best for your product.

Do I need to send the product?

Usually yes. A creator who really uses the product makes a more honest and convincing video.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Agree upfront how many rounds of changes are included, so both sides know what to expect.
