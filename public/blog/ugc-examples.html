How to pick the right format

  • New product? Start with unboxing and tutorial: people first need to understand what it is.
  • Ads? Honest review, "3 reasons why" and problem and solution often work best.
  • Filling your own TikTok or Instagram? Get ready with me and day in my life feel most like normal content.

Tip: combine formats

You don't have to choose. Get an unboxing made for your own channel, and cut a short "3 reasons" version from the same footage for your ad. One shoot, more videos.

Frequently asked questions

Which UGC format works best?

It depends on the product and goal. Test two or three formats side by side and see which performs best.

Can one creator make several formats?

Yes. Many creators film a review, an unboxing and a short ad version in one go.
