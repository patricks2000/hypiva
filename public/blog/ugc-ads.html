UGC ads: why they work and how to make them

Polished ads get scrolled past more and more. UGC ads look like a normal post, so people watch them. Here is how to use UGC well in your ads on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

Why UGC works well in ads

  • It fits the feed. A video that looks like a normal post doesn't scream ad, so people watch longer.
  • It feels honest. A real person using your product convinces faster than a brand talking about itself.
  • You can test a lot. UGC is faster and easier to make than a big shoot, so you can try several versions.

How a good UGC ad is built

  1. Hook (0 to 3 seconds). A line or image that stops the scroll. For example: "I never thought this would work".
  2. Problem. Something your audience recognises.
  3. Product in action. Show how it works, not just how it looks.
  4. Result. What is better after using it?
  5. Call to action. Short and concrete: "link in bio" or "try it yourself".

Test several hooks

The hook decides most of the result. Ask for two or three different openings per video. Then you can test several ads with the same middle part, without ordering a new video each time.

Formats that work well as ads

  • Testimonial: someone honestly shares their experience.
  • Unboxing: the first moment with your product.
  • Before and after: show the difference.
  • "3 reasons why": fast, clear and easy to follow.
  • Day in the life: your product as a natural part of the day.

Usage rights: agree on them upfront

If you want to run a UGC video as an ad, agree on it upfront with the creator or agency: which channels, how long, and whether you may cut or edit it. If you want to advertise through the creator's own account (for example with Spark Ads on TikTok), agree on that separately too.

How many UGC videos do you need for ads?

A good start is three to five videos, each with two or three hooks. That gives you ten or more versions to test. Ads wear out after a while because people see them too often, so plan new videos too.

Frequently asked questions

Does UGC work for small brands?

Especially for small brands. You don't need a big production to make an ad that works.

Does the creator also need to post on their own account?

Not necessarily. For UGC ads you usually run the video from your own account. Posting on the creator's account can be an extra if you also want reach.

How long should a UGC ad be?

Often 15 to 30 seconds. More important than length: the first three seconds have to land.
