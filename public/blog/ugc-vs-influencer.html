UGC vs influencer marketing: what is the difference?

Both use real people and short videos, so they are easy to mix up. But UGC and influencer marketing do different jobs. Here is the difference, what each one is good for, and how to choose.

The short answer

With influencer marketing you pay for reach: a creator posts about your product to their own followers. With UGC (user generated content) you pay for the video itself: a creator makes content that you use on your own channels and in your ads.

When UGC is the better choice

  • You run ads. UGC videos feel like normal posts, so they often perform better than polished commercials in TikTok and Instagram ads.
  • You need a lot of content. You can test different hooks and angles without booking a big shoot every time.
  • You want trust on your product page. A real person using the product answers questions a photo cannot.
  • Your product needs explaining. Unboxings, tutorials and "get ready with me" videos show how it really works.

When influencer marketing is the better choice

  • You want to reach a new audience fast. A creator with the right followers puts you in front of people who have never heard of you.
  • You are launching something. A wave of posts around a launch date builds buzz.
  • The creator is part of the story. Some audiences buy because they trust that specific person.

You can combine them

Many brands do both: a creator posts the video on their own account for reach, and the brand also gets the video to use in ads. Agree on this upfront, so it is clear where the video may be used and for how long.

How to choose

  1. Start with your goal. Need content for ads and socials? Go for UGC. Need new eyeballs? Go for influencers.
  2. Look at your budget. UGC is usually easier to start small with, because you pay for videos, not for audience size.
  3. Test. Start with a few videos or one creator, see what works, then scale up.

Frequently asked questions

Does a UGC creator need many followers?

No. For UGC the quality of the video matters more than the number of followers, because the video is used on your channels, not theirs.

Can I use an influencer video in my ads?

Only if you agree on it. Ask for usage rights upfront, including where and how long you can use the video.

Which one works better on TikTok?

Both work. UGC is strong for ads and your own account, influencers are strong for reaching new people. Many brands use both.
