FAQ

Questions brands ask

What kind of creators do you work with?

TikTok and UGC creators in many niches, in English, Dutch, French and more. For every brand we pick creators whose content and audience really fit your product.

What does it cost?

It depends on the number of creators, the type of video and whether the creator also posts it. Tell us what you have in mind and you get a clear price, from a small test to a bigger campaign.

Can I see the video before it goes live?

Yes. You review every video first and can ask for a change.

Are paid posts disclosed?

Yes. Paid posts are always marked as a partnership, as advertising rules require.

Can we use the video as an ad?

Yes. We agree upfront how and for how long you can use it.
