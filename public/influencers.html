How it works

From idea to video, without the hassle

Four clear steps. You always know what happens and what to expect.

01

Get to know each other

You tell us what your product is. We look honestly at whether it fits our creators. If it doesn't, we say so.

02

Brief & budget

We agree together on what you get: how many videos, which creator and the price. No surprises.

03

First version

You review the videos before you use them. Want something different? One round of changes per video is included.

04

Go live

You use the videos on your own channels or as ads. We help you see what works best.

FAQ

Questions brands ask

What kind of creators do you work with?

With a small group of creators, in Dutch, English and French. For each product we look honestly at whether it fits one of them.

What does it cost?

The price is agreed together and depends on the number of videos, the creators and how you use the videos. Tell us what you have in mind and you get a clear price upfront, from a small test to a bigger campaign.

Which languages are available?

Right now we make UGC videos in Dutch, English and French. Need another language? That's possible on request, just let us know.

What if I'm not happy with a video?

Then we talk it through together with the creator and adjust the video. One round of changes per video is always included.

Can I see the video before it goes live?

Yes. You review every video first and can ask for a change.

Can we use the video as an ad?

Yes. We agree upfront how and for how long you can use it.
